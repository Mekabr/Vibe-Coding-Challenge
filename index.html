The science behind it

Sticky mucus

In cystic fibrosis the CFTR channel is defective. Airway mucus becomes dehydrated and thick, and the cilia can barely clear it.

Early colonizer

S. aureus is one of the first and most common bacteria in CF lungs, especially in children.

Golden armour

The golden pigment staphyloxanthin – hence “aureus” – protects the bacteria from the reactive oxygen species of neutrophils.

Biofilm & iron

Inside a biofilm, S. aureus survives antibiotics and immune attacks better. The body keeps iron scarce, which makes it precious for bacteria.
